How this works and what it was trained on

The detector is the YOLOv8n model from the SWIFT-US proof-of-concept study: 3.0 M parameters, trained on 2 253 single views from 455 examinations acquired with the SAFE protocols and annotated by an orthopaedic surgeon. Under five-fold cross-validation by examination it reached an examination-level AUC of 0.97 for distal forearm fractures. The weights shown here are from one cross-validation fold.

Processing: the image is converted to greyscale, letterboxed to 640 × 640 px and passed to the network with ONNX Runtime Web (WebAssembly). Boxes are decoded, filtered at the chosen threshold and merged with non-maximum suppression (IoU 0.5). Nothing is uploaded; the page only needed the network to download the model (12 MB) and the runtime (11 MB) once.

Classes: fracture (distal forearm, proximal humerus) · joint effusion (elbow). Elbow effusion detection is weak in this model and is shown for completeness.

Expected inputs are B-mode ultrasound views like those of the training set; photographs of a screen work but with lower confidence. Other image types will produce meaningless output.
